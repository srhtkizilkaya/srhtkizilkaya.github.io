Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to all games and apps developed and published by Serhat Kızılkaya (also known as Fourty Poles Studio), including: Pyramidka: Ring Stack Puzzle, 2000 IQ! - Tricky Puzzles, Nut Out! - Screw Puzzle, Stickman: Going Home, Color Laser Pop, 2001 IQ - Tricky Brain Puzzles, DUEVO and Katre – Dhikr Drops. Sections marked ★ DUEVO apply only to the DUEVO app, and sections marked ★ KATRE apply only to the Katre app.

1. Information We Collect — Games

Our games may collect the following data automatically through third-party services:

  • Device identifiers (Advertising ID / IDFA)
  • Gameplay and app usage statistics
  • Crash reports and performance data
  • General location (country/region level only)

We do not collect names, email addresses, passwords, or any personally identifiable information directly through our games.

2. Information We Collect — ★ DUEVO

DUEVO collects the following information to provide its accounting and finance features:

Account & Identity

  • Email address, display name, and Google account ID (via Google Sign-In)

User-Entered Data

  • Chart of accounts, income and expense records
  • Company name and business details
  • Invoice images and PDF files uploaded by the user

Automatically Collected

  • Device information and app version
  • Crash reports and app usage statistics (via Firebase)
  • Subscription and purchase status (via RevenueCat)

3. How We Use Your Information — ★ DUEVO

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your account
  • To store and sync your financial records across devices
  • To process and manage your subscription
  • To improve app performance and fix bugs

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3a. Information We Collect — ★ KATRE

Katre is a dhikr counter. It works fully offline, shows no ads and contains no analytics or advertising trackers.

Kept only on your device

  • Your dhikr counts (per dhikr and per day), daily goal and streak
  • Your settings (theme, language, sound and vibration)
  • Your Premium status and, if you chose one, your profile photo

This data never leaves your phone unless you join the optional rankings or keep your profile with Google. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it from the phone.

Backup with your Google account (only if you connect Google)

  • Your dhikr counts (each dhikr’s total and daily counts), your daily goal and your water colours, so they come back on a new phone or after reinstalling. This backup is private: only you can read it, and it is never shown to other users or used for the rankings.

Optional rankings (only if you choose to join)

  • A nickname you choose (your real name is never asked for)
  • A profile picture: an ornament motif, or a photo you pick from your gallery (resized to a small 192×192 image)
  • The place you choose from a list: country, and in Türkiye your province and, optionally, district. We never use GPS or your device location.
  • Your dhikr totals, the number of dhikr counted in each period (day, week, month), your streak, the date you joined, and whether you have Premium
  • Technical records used to keep rankings fair: when each batch of counts was sent and how many were counted in that time

Account & Identity

  • A random anonymous account ID (Firebase Anonymous Authentication), created when you open the rankings
  • If you choose “Keep your profile with Google”: your Google account ID and email address (via Google Sign-In). Your email is never shown to other users.

Automatically Collected

  • Crash reports (via Firebase Crashlytics): the error, device model, operating system and app version, and a random installation ID. Crash reports contain no dhikr counts or profile data.

Device permissions

  • Photo gallery: only when you tap “Choose a photo from my phone”, and only the photo you pick
  • Ringer mode: read so that drop sounds stay silent when your phone is on silent

3b. What Other Users Can See — ★ KATRE

If you join the rankings, other Katre users can see your nickname, profile picture, chosen place, level, dhikr totals and counts in the rankings, streak, the month you joined, and a Premium badge if you have Premium. Users can report an unsuitable nickname or photo; a report records only how many times a profile was reported and when, not who reported it.

Totals for countries, provinces and districts are shown as combined sums and are not linked to any person.

3c. How We Use Your Information — ★ KATRE

  • To show the rankings and the combined totals of countries, provinces and districts
  • To keep rankings fair (unrealistically fast counting is held back)
  • To let you restore your profile and your dhikr counts on a new phone with your Google account
  • To fix crashes and improve stability

We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not share it with anyone except the service providers listed below that run the app.

3d. Data Location, Retention & Deletion — ★ KATRE

Where it is stored. Ranking data is stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions) in Belgium (europe-west1). Profile photos are stored in Firebase Cloud Storage in the United States (us-east1). Crash reports are processed by Google. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. By joining the rankings you consent to this transfer and processing outside Türkiye under Google’s safeguards.

How long. Your ranking profile is kept while you take part in the rankings, and your Google backup while your account exists. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days by Firebase Crashlytics.

Deleting your data. In the app go to Profile → Your community profile → Leave the rankings and delete my data. This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, nickname, photo, all your ranking entries, your Google backup and your account from our servers. Combined totals of countries, provinces and districts are not linked to you and remain. You can also request deletion by email at fourtypolesstudio@gmail.com; we process requests within 30 days.

KVKK (Türkiye). For users in Türkiye this section also serves as the clarification text (aydınlatma metni) under Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The data controller (veri sorumlusu) is Serhat Kızılkaya. Ranking data is processed on the basis of your explicit choice to join; crash data on the basis of our legitimate interest in a working app. You may exercise your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK by contacting fourtypolesstudio@gmail.com.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Some of our games and DUEVO offer optional in-app purchases or subscriptions. All transactions are processed securely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. We do not store payment card information on our own servers.

For issues with purchases, contact us at fourtypolesstudio@gmail.com or reach out to Apple / Google support directly.

6. Biometric Authentication ★ DUEVO

DUEVO may offer optional biometric authentication (fingerprint / Face ID) as a security feature. Biometric verification happens entirely on your device. We never collect, store, or transmit your fingerprint or face data. The app only receives a "verified / not verified" result from your device's operating system and never has access to the biometric data itself.

7. Advertising

Some of our games display ads via Google AdMob, and some use the Meta (Facebook) SDK to measure our own ad campaigns on Instagram and Facebook. You can opt out of personalized advertising:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"
  • Android: Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or enable "Opt out of Ads Personalization"

On iOS, Color Laser Pop will ask for permission before tracking, in accordance with Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized based on tracking.

DUEVO does not display any advertisements.

Katre does not display any advertisements and does not use the Advertising ID.

8. Data Retention

Analytics and crash data collected through Firebase is retained per Google's standard retention policies. For DUEVO, your account data stored in Supabase is retained as long as your account is active. Upon account deletion, your data is permanently removed within 30 days.

9. Your Rights — Data Deletion ★ DUEVO

In accordance with GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Export your data

To request account deletion or a data export, contact us at fourtypolesstudio@gmail.com. We will process your request within 30 days.

9a. Backups ★ DUEVO

To protect against data loss and ensure service continuity, we create encrypted backups of the database. Backups are stored securely, access-restricted, and kept only as long as necessary.

9b. Data Location & International Transfer ★ DUEVO

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers operated by Supabase (hosted on Amazon Web Services), which may be located outside your country, including outside Türkiye and the European Union. By using the app, you consent to the transfer and processing of your data in these locations under appropriate safeguards.

9c. KVKK (Türkiye) ★ DUEVO

For users in Türkiye, this policy also serves as the clarification text (aydınlatma metni) under Law No. 6698 (KVKK). The data controller (veri sorumlusu) is Serhat Kızılkaya. You may exercise your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK by contacting fourtypolesstudio@gmail.com.

10. Children's Privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date. Continued use of our apps after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
